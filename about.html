Our Mission

ZEEYES Books was built on a simple conviction: business owners should spend their energy inventing, selling, and hiring—not wrestling with spreadsheets and tax codes.

The ZEEYES Story

The Antidote to Black-Box Accounting & Unreliable AI Bots

In 2021, our founders—veteran Big Four CPAs and senior corporate tax advisors—watched with alarm as new tech startups attempted to automate bookkeeping using machine learning bots. The results were disastrous: hallucinated write-offs, botched sales tax filings, and hundreds of confused founders receiving IRS deficiency penalties.

We founded ZEEYES Books on an uncompromising commitment: Accounting is too critical to leave to algorithms. We built a human-first advisory firm where every general ledger, balance sheet, and corporate return is personally reconciled, verified, and signed by a licensed US CPA.
